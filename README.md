


🏥 Diagnostic Test Center Portal - Built on ServiceNow

A unified digital healthcare platform developed on ServiceNow Service Portal to automate diagnostic test booking and report distribution. It eliminates manual queues, phone bookings, and physical reports by providing a complete end-to-end online workflow for patients, technicians, and admins.

📌 Project Summary

Conventional diagnostic centers rely on long waiting lines, manual entries, slot conflicts, and hard-copy reports. This solution transforms the complete patient journey into a digital flow - from exploring tests and booking a slot to payment, sample tracking, and report download, with automated processes and strict role-based access.

🎯 Key Goals

1. Simplified Patient Experience
To offer an intuitive Service Portal where patients can explore tests/packages, pick convenient date & time, and choose between center visit or home collection.

2. Streamlined Operations
To minimize manual effort and errors by automating bookings, payment checks, status changes, and alerts through Flow Designer and Scheduled Jobs.

3. Smart Slot Management
To avoid double bookings and conflicts with live validation of available time slots at the time of booking.

4. Clear & Automated Billing
To calculate charges automatically based on selected tests, packages, home collection fees, and applicable discounts, integrated with payment confirmation.

5. Secured Data Access
To enforce strong security with role-based ACLs and patient-specific data isolation for appointments and medical reports.

✨ Core Features

🧪 Test Catalog Management: Centralized listing of diagnostic tests and health packages with pricing details

📅 Smart Appointment Booking: Step-by-step portal interface for selecting date, time, and collection type

🏠 Doorstep Sample Collection: Home collection option with dynamic form behavior

⏱️ Live Availability Check: Backend logic to block overbooking and overlapping schedules

💰 Auto Price Calculation: Real-time billing based on tests, packages, collection mode, and offers

🤖 Workflow Automation: End-to-end automation for validation, payment, and approvals using Flow Designer

🔁 Flexible Reschedule & Cancel: Built-in logic for appointment modifications and cancellation policies

🔔 Automated Email Alerts: Notifications for booking confirmation, payment, reminders, collection status, report ready, and cancellations

🗓️ Auto Status Engine: Scheduled jobs to move records through Scheduled > Sample Collected > Processing > Report Ready > Completed and mark no-shows

📄 Digital Report Handling: Technicians can upload reports and patients can securely view/download them

🛠️ Admin Dashboard: Full control to manage catalog, pricing rules, slots, staff allocation, and workflows via custom tables

🔐 Access Control: Separate ACLs for Patient, Lab Technician, and Administrator roles

👥 Roles & Responsibilities

Patient: Search tests, book slots, make payment, track appointment progress, cancel/reschedule, download reports

Lab Technician: Check assigned tasks, update sample collection & processing status, upload diagnostic reports

Admin: Configure tests, pricing, time slots, technician mapping, and control system workflows and approvals

🛠️ Technologies Used

ServiceNow Platform, Service Portal, HTML / CSS, AngularJS, Client Scripts, Server-side Scripts, UI Policies, Script Includes, GlideRecord / GlideAjax, Flow Designer, Scheduled Jobs, Access Control Rules (ACLs), Email Notifications & Events
